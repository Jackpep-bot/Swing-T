# Edge map: every strategy by the liquidity of the stock it signalled

Generated by `swing_engine.research.edge_map` from the replay shadow ledgers. Exploratory: it shows where to look, and anything it finds has been picked from thousands of cells, so only a forward test can confirm it. Each cell is one strategy's signals on stocks in one band of 20-day average dollar volume, graded like the leaderboard: net R per signal after costs, minus what SPY returned over the same days (`vs SPY`), t on non-overlapping blocks. 9707 trials are logged in all, these cells included.

Universe: config/research_smallcap.yaml (the 2,500 stocks after the 1,500 most liquid). Windows: the two halves of 2024-10-07 .. 2026-10-05. Pre-registered in docs/preregistration/2026-10-13-smallcap-map.md.

Costs are half the spread estimated from daily bars, at least 20 bp a side below $50M a day. Real spreads in the thinnest band are often wider, so its numbers flatter it.
