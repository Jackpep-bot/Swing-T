## Strategies

### [activist_13d_drift](strategies/activist_13d_drift.md) (built_disabled, evidence B)
Buy at the open of the session after an original Schedule 13D (a >5% holder declaring intent) becomes public on EDGAR, hold 10 sessions (card range 5-20), 2 ATR catastrophic stop.

### [apirine_roc_bands](strategies/apirine_roc_bands.md) (not_built, evidence D)
In an uptrend (close above an EMA), buy when smoothed ROC climbs back above its lower RMS band; exit when it falls back below the upper band or price loses the EMA.

### [ath_trend_following_wide_stop](strategies/ath_trend_following_wide_stop.md) (built_disabled (pre-registered 2026-10-09), evidence C)
Buy any liquid US stock that closes at its highest close ever, exit only on a trailing stop ten ATRs below the high; few trades, long holds, and a payoff that comes from a small share of very large winners rather than a thin per-trade edge, so per-stock costs are a small fraction of each trade.

### [avwap_pullback_shannon](strategies/avwap_pullback_shannon.md) (not_built, evidence D)
In a Stage 2 stock above a rising anchored VWAP, wait for a 2-6 day pullback on falling volume toward the rising 5-day MA / AVWAP, then buy only when strength returns (higher low, close above the prior high and the 5-day MA); stop under the dip low, first third at the R2 pivot or swing high, trail while the 5-day MA rises.

### [base_breakout](strategies/base_breakout.md) (shadow_only, evidence C)
Buy a leader (30%+ prior advance, high RS, confirmed market uptrend) on the close through the pivot of a 7-65 week cup's handle or a 5+ week flat base no more than 15% deep, on volume 40%+ above average and no more than 5% past the pivot; cut at 7-8% or back in the pattern; take 20-25%.

### [bb_bullish_engulfing_kosinski](strategies/bb_bullish_engulfing_kosinski.md) (not_built, evidence D)
Buy a bullish engulfing candle that closes above the lower Bollinger Band, with an ATR stop, only if the distance to the upper band is at least the minimum reward:risk; exit at the upper band or the stop.

### [bollinger_band_mean_reversion](strategies/bollinger_band_mean_reversion.md) (not_built, evidence D)
After a close below the lower 20-day, 2-SD Bollinger band, buy when price crosses back above that band and sell near the middle or upper band. It is a broker demonstration strategy with no published edge.

### [bollinger_pctb_mfi_trend](strategies/bollinger_pctb_mfi_trend.md) (not_built, evidence D)
Buy when price pushes into the top 20% of its 20-day Bollinger range (%b > 0.8) and volume-weighted momentum confirms (MFI > 80); stop and trail with Parabolic SAR.

### [bollinger_squeeze_breakout](strategies/bollinger_squeeze_breakout.md) (not_built, evidence C)
After Bollinger BandWidth falls to a six-month low (volatility contraction), go with the first close outside the band (Method I) or two consecutive closes outside it (Method IV), expecting a volatility expansion in that direction.

### [bollinger_w_bottom_ii_reversal](strategies/bollinger_w_bottom_ii_reversal.md) (not_built, evidence D)
A lower-band tag while volume-weighted close location (Intraday Intensity) is positive is a buy **alert**. A W-bottom is a first low at or below the lower band and a retest that holds inside the band. Buy the confirmation (a strong up bar, or a break above the middle peak of the W), stop under the retest low, and exit at the mid or upper band.

### [boomers_cooper](strategies/boomers_cooper.md) (not_built, evidence D)
In a strong trend (ADX > 30, +DI > -DI), two consecutive inside days mark a pause; buy a stop just above the second inside day's high with the stop just under its low, expecting the trend to resume.

### [breakout_52w](strategies/breakout_52w.md) (enabled, evidence C)
Buy the first close above the prior 52-week high on at least 1.5x average volume, closing near the day's high; ATR stop and a fixed-R target.

### [bull_flag_breakout](strategies/bull_flag_breakout.md) (not_built, evidence C)
Buy the break above a short, shallow, declining-volume consolidation (flag) that follows a steep advance (pole); target a fraction of the pole. This card is the generic flag, run as a comparison against the RS-filtered `qullamaggie_flag`.

### [calhoun_adx_breakout](strategies/calhoun_adx_breakout.md) (not_built, evidence D)
When ADX crosses above 40 while price is at its 15-day high, record that bar's high and buy on a stop a fixed offset above it.

### [calhoun_atr_high_sma_breakout](strategies/calhoun_atr_high_sma_breakout.md) (not_built, evidence D)
Volatility-expansion breakout: when ATR is at its 14-bar high and price is above its 100-day SMA, buy on a stop a fixed offset above that bar's high.

### [calhoun_four_day_breakout](strategies/calhoun_four_day_breakout.md) (not_built, evidence D)
In a stock above its moving average, four consecutive bullish candles followed by a buy stop a fixed offset above the pattern's highest high.

### [calhoun_mean_reversion_swing](strategies/calhoun_mean_reversion_swing.md) (not_built, evidence D)
Despite the name, a pullback-in-uptrend entry: after an up-leg of at least 20 bars and $5, price retraces about 50% of it, then a $0.50 bounce off the pullback low triggers a long.

### [canslim](strategies/canslim.md) (not_built, evidence C)
Buy the top 2-3 stocks of a leading group when they break out of a proper base, within 5% of the pivot, on volume 40-50% or more above average. Qualifying stocks show: - quarterly EPS up 18-25% or more and accelerating - 3-year EPS growth of 25% or more - ROE of 17% or more - RS rating of 80 or more - rising fund ownership

### [cci_correction](strategies/cci_correction.md) (not_built, evidence D)
Once weekly CCI(26) has pushed above +100 (bullish bias), buy when daily CCI dips below -100 and then crosses back above 0. It is a trend pullback entry timed by an oscillator.

### [chartschool_gap_first_hour](strategies/chartschool_gap_first_hour.md) (not_built, evidence D)
Classify the opening gap as full or partial, up or down. Let the first hour (to about 10:30 ET) set a range. Buy on a stop two ticks above the first-hour high, or short two ticks below the first-hour low. Exit with an 8% (long) or 4% (short) trailing stop, tighter (5-6%) for partial gaps.

### [classic_pattern_detector_lmw](strategies/classic_pattern_detector_lmw.md) (not_built, evidence C)
Replace unpublished vendor pattern engines (thinkorswim, Recognia/Trading Central, Finviz) with the published Lo-Mamaysky-Wang kernel-regression detector for 10 classic patterns, then trade breakouts from them with fixed rules.

### [composite_cost_aware_rank](strategies/composite_cost_aware_rank.md) (built_disabled (pre-registered 2026-10-09), evidence B)
Stop trading each signal as its own strategy: combine a few slow, published, already-built signals into one rank, hold the top names in one book, and only sell a name when it falls well out of the buy zone. Combining lets opposing trades cancel and the band roughly halves turnover; this is the one design the cost literature finds survives.

### [connors_3day_high_low](strategies/connors_3day_high_low.md) (not_built, evidence C)

### [connors_cvr3_vix](strategies/connors_cvr3_vix.md) (built_disabled, evidence C)

### [darvas_box](strategies/darvas_box.md) (not_built, evidence C)

### [donchian_channel_breakout](strategies/donchian_channel_breakout.md) (not_built, evidence D)

### [earnings_announcement_premium](strategies/earnings_announcement_premium.md) (not_built, evidence B)
Stocks earn abnormally high returns in the month they are expected to report earnings; buying expected announcers (and underweighting the rest) earned more than 60 bp/month, about 7%+ a year, largest for stocks with high past announcement-period volume.

### [earnings_announcement_return_abr](strategies/earnings_announcement_return_abr.md) (not_built, evidence B)

### [earnings_seasonality](strategies/earnings_seasonality.md) (built_disabled (pre-registered 2026-10-09), evidence B)
Firms whose upcoming fiscal quarter has historically been their best quarter earn abnormal returns in the month they announce it, because investors anchor on the most recent (seasonally weaker) quarters and are surprised by a predictable seasonal jump. One position per firm per year, held around a known date.

### [eighty_twenty_reversal](strategies/eighty_twenty_reversal.md) (not_built, evidence D)

### [elder_ma_penetration_channel](strategies/elder_ma_penetration_channel.md) (not_built, evidence none)

### [expansion_pivot_cooper](strategies/expansion_pivot_cooper.md) (not_built, evidence D)

### [faber_sector_rotation](strategies/faber_sector_rotation.md) (not_built, evidence C)

### [heikin_ashi_trend_ride](strategies/heikin_ashi_trend_ride.md) (not_built, evidence D)

### [heston_sadka_seasonality](strategies/heston_sadka_seasonality.md) (not_built, evidence B)

### [high_turnover_short_term_momentum](strategies/high_turnover_short_term_momentum.md) (not_built, evidence B)
Last month's winners keep winning next month when they traded on very high share turnover; low-turnover stocks show the classic one-month reversal. For the engine: a big 1-month move on high turnover is a continuation candidate, not a fade.

### [high_volume_return_premium](strategies/high_volume_return_premium.md) (built_disabled (pre-registered 2026-10-10), evidence B)
