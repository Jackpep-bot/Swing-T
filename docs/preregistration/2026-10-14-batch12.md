## Expectation, stated before the run
Card 1: 3-6% a year, beta 0.2-0.4, a smaller deepest drop than SPY; alpha against SPY likely negative while SPY made
about 13-16% a year. Card 2: return below batch 9's replica, tracking error 6-8% a year, alpha 0 to +3% a year with t
under 1.5 pooled. **Expected result: both FAIL.**

## Store
`config/prereg_b12.yaml` -> `data/live/replay_b12.duckdb`: batch 9's store plus Alpaca SIP daily bars 2016-01-04 ..
2024-10-06 for EFA, EEM, VNQ, IEF, DBC and UUP (`scripts/extend_research_store.py`; same free source as SVXY, IWD,

unit-error guard; `sic2` from the store's `sic` table (current codes, a known point-in-time gap). Before 2024-10-07 the
store holds names still liquid in 2024 plus the delisted ingest (survivorship bias in card 2's favour).

## Card 1: `multi_asset_trend_long_flat`
1. Assets: SPY, EFA, EEM, VNQ, IEF, TLT, GLD, DBC, UUP. Signal day: each month's last session; orders at the next open.
2. ON when the close is above the close 252 sessions earlier. sigma = sample standard deviation of the last 63 daily
   returns x sqrt(252). Weight (0.10 / 9) / sigma for ON assets, capped at 0.30; if the weights add up to more than 1
   they are scaled to 1. The rest is held in BIL.
3. Reading fixed here: 2016 has 252 sessions, so the first signal day with a 252-session return is 2017-01-31 (the
   card said 2016-12-30); the book starts with the first order on 2017-02-01, and window A starts there.

## Card 2: `industry_neutral_momentum_sp500`
1. Reference days: last session of February, May, August, November. Orders at the open of the first session after
   the third Friday of the following month.
2. Universe: the 500 largest by `mcap_pit` with an as-traded close >= $10 and at least 273 sessions of bars.
3. Raw = close 21 sessions before the reference day / close 273 sessions before - 1. Score = raw minus the median raw
   of its `sic2` industry among the 500; industries with fewer than 5 members (and names with no code) form one
   "other" group.
4. Holdings: current holdings ranked in the top 150 stay (at most 100); then the highest scores until 100. Weight 1%
   each; any shortfall in SPY. Before the first book (March 2017) the book is 100% SPY. No stops; delisting at the
   last close x the store's multiplier.
5. Descriptive: tracking error against SPY over the pooled window, and batch 9's replica on the same pooled window
   (not re-graded). CZ check: not run; it needs a downloaded file from openassetpricing.com, which this session does
   not fetch without the owner's word (the card asked for it only if possible).

## Windows, costs and grading (docs/gates.md: Full-period test, Bill rate)
Three windows, each its own simulation: **A** 2017-01-01 .. 2024-10-04, **B** 2024-10-07 .. 2026-10-05, **pooled**
2017-01-01 .. 2026-10-05; a window starts with the latest book set on or before its first session. Costs:
`weights_book.simulate` (dollars traded x `research.costs` per side, at least 10 bp). PASS on either route: (1) in
both A and B, haircut Sharpe > 0, alpha against SPY > 0 and alpha t >= the card's bar; or (2) the same in the pooled
window with alpha above zero in each of A and B. Each card logs one trial (`prereg-b12`) with its pooled statistics.
Card 1 also reports the correlation of its monthly returns with SPY's.
