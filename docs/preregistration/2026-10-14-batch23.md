## Runs (all on `config/prereg_b9.yaml`, price-only, windows A, B and pooled as the replica's own grading)
1. Timing: reference-month pairs Jan/Jul, Feb/Aug (= R), Mar/Sep, Apr/Oct, May/Nov, Jun/Dec, each trading at the open
   of the first session after the third Friday of the following month, every other rule unchanged; SPY until each
   variant's first book. The six-tranche book: 1/6 of capital in each variant, never rebalanced between tranches.
2. Neighbouring settings, one change at a time from R: 50 names (enter 40, stay 60); 150 names (enter 120, stay 180);
   6-1 month look-back (from 7 months before the reference day to 1 month before); no volatility scaling (raw return);
   no score tilt (weights = `mcap_pit`, same caps, same names); quarterly (Feb / May / Aug / Nov references).
3. Cost stress: R at 2x and 3x the per-symbol cost table.
4. Stationary block bootstrap (Politis-Romano) of R's paired daily (book, SPY, BIL) returns in the pooled window:
   mean block 21 sessions, 10,000 draws, seed 20261014; annual alpha of (r - BIL) on (SPY - BIL) per draw; report the
   5th / 50th / 95th percentiles and the share of draws with alpha <= 0.
5. Total return: R and the six-tranche book's orders on `config/prereg_b9_tr.yaml`, descriptive.

## Reading rule (the card's, fixed before the run; pooled alphas)
- **ROBUST**: six-tranche alpha >= +2.45% a year, at least 5 of 6 timing variants above 0, and at least 5 of 6 settings
  above 0.
- **FRAGILE**: six-tranche alpha under +1% a year, or 3 or more timing variants at or below 0.
- **MIXED**: anything else.
If FRAGILE, the weekly report says the shadow book's backtest likely overstates it.

## Expectation (the card's)
Timing variants' pooled alpha about +1% to +6%, six-tranche about +2.5-4%, cost stress -0.3 to -0.6% a year at 3x;
likely MIXED or ROBUST.
