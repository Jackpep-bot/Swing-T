## Expectation, stated before the run
Large: alpha 0 to +1% a year, tracking error 2-3%, likely FAIL on the t bar. Mid: alpha against SPY likely negative in

## Store and inputs
`config/prereg_b9.yaml` -> `data/live/replay_b9.duckdb` (the live store as of 2026-10-13 plus ETF history). Price-only
bars, as before. Before 2024-10-07 the store holds names still liquid in 2024 plus the delisted ingest.
- `mcap_pit`, as-traded close and `net_share_issuance` (12-month change in split-adjusted shares, from the 126- and
  378-session-old filings) on the reference day: `data.fundamentals.share_issuance_features`, with batch 9's guard
  against share-count unit errors (a name above 10,000 x its 63-session median dollar volume is left out that day).
- Asset growth, accruals, gross profit / assets and ROA per filing: `data.b5_fundamentals.event_values` (new
  `MISPRICING_COLUMNS`, tested by hand): assets / assets four fiscal quarters earlier - 1; (TTM net income - TTM
  operating cash flow) / mean of the two assets; TTM gross profit / assets; TTM net income / assets; net income =
  TTM diluted EPS x the filing's cover-page shares (batch 5's proxy). A name uses its latest filing usable on the
  reference day (`avail_ts` on or before it), which must carry total assets and be usable within the last 400
  sessions.

## The rules (module docstring has the detail)
1. Reference days: last session of February, May, August, November. Orders at the open of the first session after
   the third Friday of the next month. First rebalance March 2017 (2017-03-20); before it the book is 100% SPY.
2. Universe on the reference day: as-traded close >= $5 and the filing rule above; ranked by `mcap_pit` among those;
   card 2 also needs median 63-session dollar volume >= $1M (applied after the rank).
3. Signals turned into within-universe percentiles with 1 = worst: net share issuance high, asset growth high,
   accruals high, gross profit / assets low, ROA low. Score = mean of the available percentiles; a name needs at
   least 3. Reading fixed here: a name with fewer than 3 has no score and stays in the book.
4. Excluded: the `worst` highest scores, plus names excluded last time that still rank within the worst `buffer`.
5. Weights: the rest at `mcap_pit` weight, capped per name (the excess spread pro rata), set at the rebalance and
   then left to drift. No stops; delisting at the last close x the store's multiplier.

## Windows, costs and grading (docs/gates.md: Full-period test, Bill rate)
Three simulations: **A** 2017-01-01 .. 2024-10-04, **B** 2024-10-07 .. 2026-10-05, **pooled** 2017-01-01 ..
2026-10-05; each starts with the latest book on or before its first session. Costs: `weights_book.simulate`.
PASS on either route: (1) in both A and B, haircut Sharpe > 0, alpha against SPY > 0 and alpha t >= 2.241; or (2) the
same in the pooled window with alpha above zero in each of A and B. Each card logs one trial (`prereg-b13`).
Descriptive, not criteria: tracking error against SPY; the excluded names cap-weighted; and each card's whole
universe cap-weighted with no exclusion (the card asked for it for the mid card; it is reported for both).
