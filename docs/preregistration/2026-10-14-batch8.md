## Store and the deal flag
`config/prereg_b8.yaml` -> `data/live/replay_b7.duckdb` (batch 7's research store) plus table `form_parties`
(`scripts/build_b8_store.py`): the header (`.hdr.sgml` on sec.gov) of each of the 1,057 DEFM14A and SC TO-T filings
in `form_events`, giving the SUBJECT-COMPANY CIK (530 filings) and the FILED-BY / FILER CIKs. The card assumed
`form_events` held filer and subject CIKs; it does not (a filing sits in the submissions list of every company it
names, and the accession prefix is usually a filing agent), so the header is how the card's rule is applied. Same
data source (EDGAR), nothing new to approve.

`data.b8.join_b8` (only on a store with `form_parties`) marks a deal candidate for a symbol at session s when
- (a) an SC TO-T names the symbol's CIK as subject and another CIK as filer (anchor a = s), or
- (b) a DEFM14A lists the symbol's CIK as filer and an original 8-K with Item 1.01 of the same symbol has its
  reaction session in [s - 120, s] sessions (anchor a = the earliest such 8-K);
and no earlier candidate of the symbol came within 365 calendar days (by filing date). Rows on s and the 20 sessions
after it carry `deal_v2_days` and `deal_v2_anchor`.
Known limits, accepted now: both a target and an acquirer file a DEFM14A and an 8-K 1.01 when their own holders vote,
so (b) leans on the price checks to tell them apart; the one-per-365-days rule is applied to candidates, not to
signals (a candidate that never pins still blocks the year).

## The rule (module docstring has the detail)
On a candidate's session and each of the next 20 until the first pass:
- premium: close >= 1.10 x the close 25 sessions before the anchor;
- pinned: sample standard deviation of the last 10 daily returns <= 1.0%;
the first passing session is the signal, with `mcap_pit` >= $300M and `med_dv_63` >= $5M that day. Buy the next open.
Exits: the deal closes and the symbol stops trading (the replay's delisting exit at the last close; a merger delisting
has no haircut); the break stop at 85% of the signal close (filled during the session or at the open on a gap; the
card reads the close); 180 sessions. Book `config/prereg_b8_merger_target_spread_capture_v2.yaml`: 15 positions,
1% of equity at risk on the 15% stop, capped at 6.67% per name. Session counts inside the window use the symbol's
own bars.

## Pass rule (2 trials)
PASS needs, in both windows:
1. haircut Sharpe > 0 and net R per trade > 0;
2. alpha against buy-and-hold SPY > 0 with alpha t >= `prereg_eval.alpha_t_critical(2)` (about 2.24);
3. **completed deals (delisting exits) above 50% of closed trades** (trades still open at a window's end are not
   closed), so a win from mislabelled non-targets does not count. Computed from the replay's trade list.
