## 2. `large_cap_net_repurchasers` (Pontiff & Woodgate 2008; Fama & French 2008)

Module `swing_engine/strategies/large_cap_net_repurchasers.py`; data `data.fundamentals.join_share_issuance`
(joined in the replay only); feature `big_net_issuance` in `features/extra.py`.

| Rule | Value | Source / why |
|---|---|---|
| Rebalance | last NYSE session of each month (`month_end` = 1); fills next open | card |
| Share count | cover-page `dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` (classes summed) of the latest 10-Q / 10-K, usable from the session after its filed date (`fundamental_events.avail_ts`) | card says "filed on or before d"; the engine's convention is one session later |
| Split adjustment | adjusted shares = filed count x product of the store `splits` ratios with ex_date after the filed date (units of the store's split-adjusted bars) | card intent. **Reading of the card:** its formula "x split factor between the filing and d" would, taken literally for the two dates separately, turn a split between them into an issuance; both counts are put in the same units instead. Only splits dated before the later count's filing enter the ratio (later ones cancel), so it is point-in-time. A split between the cover-page "as of" date and the filed date is treated as not yet in the count |
| Signal | NS = ln(adjusted shares usable 126 sessions ago / usable 378 sessions ago) (`NSI_NEAR_SESSIONS`, `NSI_FAR_SESSIONS`; NYSE sessions) | card / Chen-Zimmermann ShareIss1Y |
| Guard | NS is void while a filing-to-filing change of adjusted shares above **+100%** or below **-50%** lies between the two dates; both counts required | card. The join logs flagged filings and symbols (`share_issuance_guard`) |
| Universe | each session: the **500** largest rows by market cap (close x adjusted shares = as-traded price x as-traded shares), then as-traded close >= **$10** and `hist_bars` >= **504**; NS must exist | card. **Deviation:** the 500 are taken among every symbol in the replay panel (names admitted by the universe screen at some point in the window), not that session's screen; the rank below is then recomputed among that session's point-in-time universe members |
| Buy zone | percentile rank of NS (`big_net_issuance_rank`, low = largest net reduction) <= `buy_rank_max` = **0.10** on a rebalance session | card |
| Hold zone / exit | on a rebalance session, sell at the next open when the rank is > `hold_rank_max` = **0.20** or missing (left the 500, price, or lost its share counts / hit the guard) | card |
| Stop | `stop_atr_mult` = **3** x `atr_63` below the signal close, static | card (catastrophe stop; the engine sizes on it) |
| Target, regime gate, time stop, engine trail | none; `engine_trail = False` | card |
| Weighting | equal weight through the book: 2% of equity per name, at most 50 names (below) | card. A stopped-out or sold name can be bought again at a later month end; cash is not rebalanced between holdings |
| Same-day ranking | `score` = 1 - rank (largest net reduction first) | engine choice for the slot race |
| Columns read | close, `big_net_issuance`, `big_net_issuance_rank`, `month_end`, `atr_63` | |

## 3. `volatility_managed_spy` (Moreira & Muir, JF 2017)

Module `swing_engine/strategies/volatility_managed_spy.py`; feature `rvar_22` in `features/extra.py`.

| Rule | Value | Source / why |
|---|---|---|
| Instrument | `symbols` = **SPY** | card |
| RV | `rvar_22` = sum of squared daily close-to-close log returns over the last **22** sessions, read at the latest month-end session on or before the signal day | card |
| Target weight | w = min(`max_weight` **1.0**, c / RV), c = `target_variance` = 0.16^2 / 12 = **0.002133** | card (fixed in advance, no fitting) |
| No-trade band | rebalance only when \|w_new - w bought\| >= `rebalance_band` = **0.10**, checked at each month-end close | card. "w held" is the weight at entry, not the drifted weight |
| Cash | earns zero | card |
| Target, regime gate, engine trail | none; `engine_trail = False` | card |

**How the fraction is held (engine approximation, stated before results).** The engine sizes by stop distance and
keeps one position per symbol with no partial adds or sells. The card names no discrete approximation, so the
closest version the engine can express is registered:

- **Size.** The sizer buys equity x risk% / (1.01 x close - stop) shares. The signal's stop is placed at
  close x (1.01 - 0.20 / w) with the book's `risk_per_trade_pct` = 20 (`book_risk_pct`, tested equal to the
  book), so the position is w x equity in whole shares. The stop is 19% below the close at w = 1 and further
  away for smaller w. It is a real resting stop: an **engine addition** (card: no stop).
- **Weight floor.** A weight below 0.20 has no positive stop. `min_weight` = **0.20**: 0.10 <= w < 0.20 is held
  as 0.20; w < 0.10 is cash. This only matters when 22-day realized volatility is above about 36% annualised.
- **Rebalance = full round trip.** When the band is breached at a month-end close the whole position is sold at
  the next open and the new weight is bought at the open after that (the strategy emits an entry signal every
  session at the current target; the engine ignores it while SPY is held). So a rebalance pays costs on the whole
  position, not on the traded difference, and spends one session in cash. Both work against the strategy relative
  to the card.
- The same every-session signal opens the first position (signal at the window's first close, fill at its
  second open) and re-enters at the second open after a stop-out.

## Books (fixed now, not changed after any run)

One book cannot serve both a 50-name equal-weight portfolio and a 100% SPY position (the sizer has one
`max_position_pct` and one `risk_per_trade_pct`), and SPY is an ETF, which the screened common-stock universe
excludes (`include_etfs: false`). So the group has two settings files, both extending `replay.yaml`, $100,000 start:

| | `config/prereg_b2.yaml` (`large_cap_net_repurchasers`) | `config/prereg_b2_spy.yaml` (the two SPY strategies) |
|---|---|---|
| Universe | replay universe (price >= $5, ADV >= $5M, top 1,500 by dollar volume, as-traded, refreshed every 5 sessions) | `static_symbols: [SPY]` |
| `max_position_pct` | 2.0 (equal weight 1/50) | 101.0 |
| `risk_per_trade_pct` | 0.5 (binds only for a stop further than 24% away; otherwise the 2% cap sizes) | 20.0 |
| `max_open_positions` | 50 | 1 |
| `max_new_orders_per_day` | 50 | 1 |

Why 101 and 20 for SPY: the sizer divides by its marketable limit (close + 1%), so a 100% cap would buy 99% of
equity and leave a permanent 1% cash drag against the benchmark; 101% asks for equity / close shares and the fill
is then cut to the cash on hand (`research.backtest._fill_entry`), so the position can never exceed 100% of
equity and there is no leverage. 20% risk makes the cap, not the stop, size `fomc_cycle_even_weeks` (20 / 16 > 1)
and lets `volatility_managed_spy` express weights from 0.20 to 1.0 with a stop no closer than 19%. Whole shares
leave up to one SPY share (under 0.7% of equity) in cash. No sector cap applies in the replay.

## How it is graded

1. Portfolio replay of each strategy ALONE, its own exits, no router, through `research.prereg_run` (one process
   per strategy and window, each on its own copy of the store; identical to serial runs):

   ```
   nohup uv run python -m swing_engine.research.prereg_run --settings config/prereg_b2.yaml \
     --benchmark-settings config/prereg_b2_spy.yaml --tag-prefix prereg-b2- \
     --slugs fomc_cycle_even_weeks,large_cap_net_repurchasers,volatility_managed_spy --n-trials 3 \
     --benchmark SPY --benchmark-slugs fomc_cycle_even_weeks,volatility_managed_spy \
     --out docs/preregistration/2026-10-10-batch2-results.md > data/logs/research/prereg_b2.log 2>&1 &
   ```

   `--benchmark-settings` is the settings file for the replays of the `--benchmark-slugs`. Positions open at a
   window's end are closed at its last close.
2. Costs, as in `2026-10-10-two-picks.md`: the replay's `CostModel` defaults (10 bp per side slippage, SEC fee and
   FINRA TAF on sales) plus the per-stock top-up from `research.costs.cost_table` (Abdi-Ranaldo half-spread, floor
   10 / 20 bp) above the 10 bp already charged, booked on the exit date (`research.prereg_eval.grade_run`). SPY
   pays the same per-stock model (floor 10 bp per side).
3. Absolute columns per strategy and window, unchanged: trades, net R per trade, its t-stat, annualised Sharpe of
   the net daily equity curve, haircut Sharpe and deflated Sharpe (n_trials = 3), max drawdown, holding days,
   top-7% P&L share, return.
4. Against buy-and-hold SPY (grader addition, written and tested before any replay): daily excess return = the
   strategy's net daily return minus SPY's close-to-close return from the store's bars on the same sessions.
   Reported for all three: excess return per year (mean x 252), information ratio (annualised Sharpe of the excess
   returns), its Harvey-Liu haircut with n_trials = 3, and for information the OLS alpha per year, its t-stat
   (residual s.d. / sqrt(n)), beta, average exposure and SPY's own Sharpe.

## Multiple testing

Harvey-Liu Bonferroni haircut (`research.metrics.haircut_sharpe`) with **n_trials = 3** on each window's annual net
Sharpe and on each window's information ratio; deflated Sharpe with n_trials = 3 is reported, not a criterion.
With n = 3 a positive haircut needs p_adj = 3p < 1, i.e. t > 0.967: an annual Sharpe or IR above about 0.69 in
window A (500 sessions) and about 0.35 in window B (1,952 sessions). The replays are also logged to the trial log.

## Pass criteria (each strategy on its own)

- `fomc_cycle_even_weeks`, `volatility_managed_spy`: PASS only if the **haircut information ratio against SPY is
  positive in window A AND in window B**. The absolute Sharpe and net R per trade are reported, not criteria (R is
  measured against an arbitrary catastrophe stop, and anything long SPY has a positive Sharpe in a rising market).
- `large_cap_net_repurchasers`: PASS only if the haircut Sharpe is positive in window A AND window B, and net R
  per trade > 0 in both (the standard rule). Its excess return, IR and alpha against SPY are reported beside it.

Otherwise FAIL, recorded in the cards and `docs/STATUS.md`. A pass goes to the walk-forward skill and gates.md; it
does not enable anything.

## Known limits (stated before results)

- **The yardstick is harsher than the cards'.** The two SPY cards name the alpha t-stat from a regression on SPY
  (beta free) as primary. The registered criterion is the excess return over buy-and-hold SPY (beta fixed at 1).
  `fomc_cycle_even_weeks` is in the market about half the time, so in a rising market it must earn the whole SPY
  return in half the sessions to pass; the regression alpha and t-stat are in the report so the two readings can
  be compared, but only the haircut IR decides.
- Power is low: about 16 meetings and 24 month ends in window A.
- FOMC: sessions are counted on the calendar as it turned out. An ad-hoc closure (2018-12-05, 2025-01-09) shifts
  later day counts of that cycle by one and was not known when the cycle began. The 2016-2020 dates were not
  re-fetched from the Fed site today. Entry and exit are next-open, not the paper's close-to-close.
- Vol-managed: the 19%+ stop, the weight floor and the full-round-trip rebalance described above; the published
  real-time evidence is negative (card), so this is close to a control.
- Repurchasers: window B is survivorship-biased before 2024-10-07; names without 10-Q / 10-K cover-page counts
  (foreign filers, many ADRs) never enter the 500; a restated count or a multi-class filer that reports classes
  inconsistently can move NS (the guard only catches steps beyond +100% / -50%); the long leg is graded on raw
  returns with the SPY comparison beside it; equal weight is by slot, not rebalanced; the 3 x ATR(63) stop is the
  card's own engine addition.
- The share join (`mcap_pit`, `close_as_traded`, `net_share_issuance`) runs in the replay only; the strategy is
  silent in the CLI scan and the nightly.
