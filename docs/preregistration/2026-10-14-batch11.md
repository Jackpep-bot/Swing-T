# Pre-registration: batch 11 (two weights cards, first batch under the full-period test)

Committed 2026-10-14 **before** any simulation of these rules exists on real data. The module was written and tested
on synthetic data only. Nothing below may change after the first run except a harness-only amendment (a bug that
stops the rules as written from running), written into this file before results are read.

Cards: `docs/proposals/swing-methods-2026-10/batch11/` (branch `claude/batch11-cards` @ 4193e31; docs-only update of c4016a5). Results that existed
when they were chosen: batches 5-10 all FAIL. Both are WEIGHTS cards of new families, simulated by
`swing_engine/research/b11_books.py` on `research.weights_book` and graded by `research.b9_books.grade`.

| # | Card | Holds | Trades |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `dividend_increase_drift` | top-1,500 names with a dividend rise or start in the last 63 sessions, equal weight capped at 4% | weekly |
| 2 | `russell_recon_migrant_drift` | names that fell from ranks 1-1,000 to 1,001-1,300 by market cap, equal weight capped at 3% | yearly, after the last Friday of June |

Both hold SPY with any weight the cap leaves unused (and card 2 holds SPY before its first cohort), never cash or
bills, so neither can lose to SPY merely by sitting out.

## Expectation, stated before the run
Card 1: alpha against SPY 0 to +2% a year with t under 1.5. Card 2: alpha around zero with t under 1.
**Expected result: both FAIL.**

## Store
`config/prereg_b9.yaml` -> `data/live/replay_b9.duckdb` (batch 9's store: the live store as of 2026-10-13 plus ETF
history; table `dividends` from the Alpaca corporate-actions feed, 2016-01 on). Price-only bars: dividends are credited
neither to the books nor to SPY. Before 2024-10-07 the store holds names still liquid in 2024 plus the delisted
ingest (survivorship bias in the books' favour). Market caps are `mcap_pit` (close x point-in-time EDGAR shares)
with batch 9's guard against share-count unit errors (a name above 10,000 x its 63-session median dollar volume
is left out that day).

## Card 1: `dividend_increase_drift`
1. Regular dividends only (`special` and `foreign` false), split-adjusted rate `rate_adj` (`data.dividends.mapped`);
   the event day is the ex-date (a session; a row whose ex-date is not a session is not an event).
2. Increase: `rate_adj` >= 1.10 x and <= 3.0 x the symbol's previous regular `rate_adj` (the latest earlier ex-date
   within 400 sessions).
3. Initiation: no regular ex-date in the 400 sessions before, at least 400 sessions of the symbol's bars before the
   ex-date, and an ex-date on or after 2017-08-01 (before that the feed's start cannot be told from a first dividend).
4. Eligible: on the ex-date, in the top 1,500 by `mcap_pit` and with an as-traded close >= $5.
5. On the last session of each week: hold every eligible event whose ex-date is in the 63 sessions ending that day,
   equal weight 1/N capped at 4%, the rest in SPY; traded at the next session's open. No stops; a holding past its last
   bar is sold at its last close x the store's delisting multiplier.
6. Descriptive, not trials: the same book with increases only, and with initiations only.

## Card 2: `russell_recon_migrant_drift`
1. Rank day: the last session of April each year (the 2024 move to semiannual reviews is ignored). Ranks: by
   `mcap_pit` among names with an as-traded close >= $1 that day (1 = largest).
2. Migrant in year Y: ranked 1-1,000 on year Y-1's rank day and 1,001-1,300 on year Y's.
3. Book: equal weight 1/N capped at 3%, the rest in SPY, set at the open of the first session after the last Friday
   of June of year Y and held until next year's book replaces it (a name in both books is not traded). The first
   cohort is June 2017 (the store's first rank day is April 2016); before it the book is 100% SPY. No stops; delisting
   as card 1.
4. Descriptive, not trials: the promoted side (1,001-1,300 last year, 1-1,000 this year) and the cohort size per year.

## Windows, costs and grading (docs/gates.md: Full-period test, Bill rate)
- Three windows, each its own simulation: **A** 2017-01-01 .. 2024-10-04, **B** 2024-10-07 .. 2026-10-05, and
  **pooled** 2017-01-01 .. 2026-10-05. A window starts with the latest book set on or before its first session.
- Costs: `weights_book.simulate`: dollars traded x the symbol's `research.costs` cost (at least 10 bp a side).
- Daily net returns vs SPY's close-to-close returns; alpha from the regression of (book - BIL) on (SPY - BIL).
- Trials: **2** (one per card, two new families). Bar: `prereg_eval.alpha_t_critical(2)` = 2.241. Each card logs one
  trial (`prereg-b11`) holding its pooled-window statistics.

## Pass rule
PASS on either route:
1. in both A and B: haircut Sharpe > 0, alpha against SPY > 0 and alpha t >= 2.241; or
2. in the pooled window: the same three conditions, with alpha against SPY above zero in each of A and B.
A pass earns a forward test (shadow ledger first), as for every card.
