## 1. `large_cap_gross_profitability` (Novy-Marx 2013)

| Rule | Value | Source / why |
|---|---|---|
| Signal | GP/A = `gross_prof` = TTM gross profit (4 consecutive quarters spanning <= 300 days) / latest total assets, as of the latest usable filing (`data.fundamentals.gross_profitability_asof` through `join_edgar`) | card |
| Universe | the 500 largest, minus names with no GP/A (fewer than 4 visible quarters of `GrossProfit`, or no positive total assets). That is the card's stand-in for "financial" (no SIC codes in the store); never filled with revenue. No price or history filter | card. | Buy zone | rank > `buy_rank_min` = **2/3** (top third) on a rebalance session | card |

| Name cap | 60 (book) | card. The buy zone (about 130 names) is larger than the cap: slots are filled best GP/A first, so the book holds roughly the 60 highest-GP/A names it could buy and replaces one only when it leaves the top half or stops out |

## 2. `large_cap_residual_momentum` (Blitz, Huij & Martens 2011)

Module `swing_engine/strategies/large_cap_residual_momentum.py`; feature `big_ff3_resid_mom_756_231` in `features/extra.py`.

| Rule | Value | Source / why |
|---|---|---|
| Score | `ff3_resid_mom_756_231`: OLS of daily excess returns (r - `ff_rf`) on `ff_mkt_rf`, `ff_smb`, `ff_hml` over **756** bars; score = sum of the last **231** residuals / their population s.d.; both windows end at the last bar of month t-2 (BHM's t-12 .. t-2 and the French publication lag), all bars and factor days required | card (registered deviation from BHM's 36 monthly regressions) |
| Universe | the 500 largest, then as-traded close >= **$10** (`close_as_traded`) and `hist_bars` >= **756** (the fit window); the score must exist | card |
| Buy zone | rank > `buy_rank_min` = **0.90** (top 10%) on a rebalance session | card |
| Hold zone / exit | on a rebalance session, sell at the next open when the rank is <= `hold_rank_min` = **0.80** or missing while the name has a raw score (left the 500 or the price floor) | card |
| Missing factors | a name with no raw `ff3_resid_mom_756_231` on a rebalance session is neither bought nor sold (the rebalance is skipped); the CAPM proxy is never used | card. With the factor table missing every name has no raw score, so the whole rebalance is skipped. A held name's own raw score is otherwise only missing if its bars have a gap |
| Name cap | 50 (book) | card |
| Replay warm-up | `warmup_calendar_days` = **1200** calendar days of bars before the window's first session | **engine addition.** The replay's default 400-day warm-up leaves a 756-bar fit NaN until three years into a window (found on the catalog `residual_momentum`, whose 2024-26 replay only fired on 2026-10-01). 757 bars are about 1,100 days, plus the two-month lag |
| Columns read | close, `ff3_resid_mom_756_231`, `big_ff3_resid_mom_756_231`, `big_ff3_resid_mom_756_231_rank`, `month_end`, `atr_63` | |

## How it is graded

1. Portfolio replay of each strategy ALONE, its own exits, no router, through `research.prereg_run` (one process
   per strategy and window, each on its own copy of the store):

   ```
   nohup uv run python -m swing_engine.research.prereg_run --settings config/prereg_b3.yaml \
     --book large_cap_gross_profitability=config/prereg_b3_gp.yaml --tag-prefix prereg-b3- \
     --slugs large_cap_gross_profitability,large_cap_residual_momentum --n-trials 2 \
     --benchmark SPY --alpha-slugs large_cap_gross_profitability,large_cap_residual_momentum \
     --out docs/preregistration/2026-10-10-batch3-results.md > data/logs/research/prereg_b3.log 2>&1 &
   ```

   `--book slug=file` is the settings file for that slug's replays; `--alpha-slugs` goes to the grader. Positions
   open at a window's end are closed at its last close.
2. Costs, as in batch 2: the replay's `CostModel` defaults (10 bp per side slippage, SEC fee and FINRA TAF on sales)
   plus the per-stock top-up from `research.costs.cost_table` (Abdi-Ranaldo half-spread, floor 10 / 20 bp) above
   the 10 bp already charged, booked on the exit date (`research.prereg_eval.grade_run`).
3. Absolute columns per strategy and window, unchanged: trades, net R per trade, its t-stat, annualised Sharpe of
   the net daily equity curve, haircut Sharpe and deflated Sharpe (n_trials = 2), max drawdown, holding days,
   top-7% P&L share, return.
4. Against buy-and-hold SPY, unchanged: excess return per year, information ratio and its haircut, OLS alpha per
   year, alpha t, beta, exposure, SPY's Sharpe. New column (grader addition, written and tested before any replay):
   one-way turnover per year = half of (bought + sold notional) over mean equity (the residual momentum card asks
   for it).
5. Alpha (grader addition `--alpha-slugs`, written and tested before any replay): OLS of the strategy's net daily
   return (after the cost top-up) on SPY's close-to-close return from the store's bars on the same sessions, over
   every session of the window, intercept and beta free. alpha t = intercept / (residual s.d. / sqrt(n)): plain
   OLS, no autocorrelation correction.

## Multiple testing

n_trials = **2** throughout. Harvey-Liu Bonferroni haircut (`research.metrics.haircut_sharpe`) on each window's
annual net Sharpe; deflated Sharpe is reported, not a criterion. The alpha rule uses the two-sided Bonferroni
critical value at a 5% family level: p <= 0.05 / 2, i.e. **alpha t >= 2.241** (normal quantile 0.9875;
`prereg_eval.alpha_t_critical(2)`). In annual terms that is an alpha of about 1.59 residual s.d. per year in
window A (500 sessions) and 0.81 in window B (1,952 sessions). The replays are also logged to the trial log.

## Pass criteria (each strategy on its own)

PASS only if, in window A AND in window B:

- the standard rule holds: haircut Sharpe > 0 and net R per trade > 0; and
- alpha vs SPY > 0 with alpha t >= 2.241.

Otherwise FAIL, recorded in the cards and `docs/STATUS.md`. A window that is "cannot test" is a FAIL for passing
purposes. A pass goes to the walk-forward skill and gates.md; it does not enable anything.

The cards say "positive haircut alpha"; the registered alpha rule is stricter than a positive Harvey-Liu haircut
(which needs only t > 0.67 with two trials) and is the binding test, as the cards intend: batch 2's repurchasers
passed the standard rule with zero alpha.

## Known limits (stated before results)

- **Low prior.** The cards expect a small positive alpha in 2017-24 and a negative one in 2024-26 for gross
  profitability, and near-zero alpha in both windows for residual momentum. A t of 2.241 on 500 sessions is a high
  bar: FAIL is the expected outcome.
- **Residual momentum is in cash at the start of window B.** The store's bars begin 2016-01-04, so the first score
  exists on rows of 2019-03 and the first buys are at the 2019-03-29 close. The alpha regression still runs over
  the whole window (2017-01-01 on), about 27 months of zero returns included; that lowers alpha and its t and is
  not corrected.
- **Residual momentum in window A needs pre-window history.** Bars before 2024-10-07 exist only for the ~4,300
  names of the Alpaca extension (liquid in 2024); a name without 757 bars by the end of month t-2 has no score.
  Nearly all of the 500 largest are in the extension, but recent large listings are left out until they have three
  years of bars.
- The window-A replay of residual momentum loads about 3.3 years of bars before the window instead of 1.1; its
  memory peak is above the 5 GB that `prereg_run` budgets for a 2024 job (not measured).
- Window B is survivorship-biased before 2024-10-07.
- Names without 10-Q / 10-K cover-page counts (foreign filers, many ADRs) never enter the 500. A restated count or
  a multi-class filer that reports classes inconsistently can move a name in or out of the 500.
- Gross profitability: "financial" is inferred from a missing `GrossProfit`; non-financial filers that do not tag
  it (some utilities, REITs, retailers reporting only cost of revenue) drop out as well, and the few financials that
  tag it stay in. A filer that stops filing keeps its last GP/A as long as it has a market cap. GP/A is updated
  with every 10-Q (TTM), not once a year in June as in the paper.
- Residual momentum uses daily-factor regressions, not BHM's monthly ones; the score is frozen for a calendar month
  (it changes on the month's first session, the buys are at the month's last close, so the score is about one month
  older than BHM's at the time of the trade).
- Both are long-only legs of long-short results; equal weight is by slot, not rebalanced; the 3 x ATR(63) stop is
  the cards' own engine addition, and net R per trade is measured against it.
- The share join (`mcap_pit`, `close_as_traded`) runs in the replay only; both strategies are silent in the CLI
  scan and the nightly.
- The alpha t-stat assumes independent daily residuals.
