# Pre-registration: 1R-target regrade of every strategy (family test) (2026-10-10)

COMMIT: <to fill>

One family of trials plus one pooled test, fixed before anything is run on real data. No replay is needed: the
signals already recorded in the replay shadow ledgers (data/live/replay_r1.duckdb, replay_r2.duckdb,
replay_news.duckdb, table `shadow_signals_replay`) are graded again with a different target and nothing else changed.

## Idea
docs/attribution.md: the typical strategy reaches about +1R of favourable excursion within 20 sessions, yet its own
target is almost never reached (0% target exits for most, about half the signals stopped). A target that is never
hit is no exit at all: the trade is a stop plus a time exit. Question: does taking profit at 1R turn the favourable
excursion into net R, or does it only cut the right tail that paid for the stops?

## Rule
- Signals: every row of the three ledgers (a duplicate `(strategy, symbol, as_of)` key is taken from the last store
  in the order r2, r1, news, as `research.cards.read_shadow` does), for EVERY base strategy: names containing `@`
  (liquidity variants), names ending `_no_news`, and the pre-registered picks (`ath_trend_following_wide_stop`,
  `composite_cost_aware_rank`, `earnings_seasonality`, `high_volume_return_premium`, `momentum_volume_early_stage`,
  and the batch 2 slugs `fomc_cycle_even_weeks`, `large_cap_net_repurchasers`, `volatility_managed_spy` if present)
  are left out. No strategy is dropped for any other reason, including having few signals.
- Variant `<slug>@t1r`: the recorded side, entry, entry type and stop are kept; the recorded target (whatever it
  was, including none) is replaced by `entry + 1.0 x (entry - stop)` using the RECORDED (planned) entry and stop.
- Grading: `research.shadow.grade_signals` (so `grade_one`), unchanged, on the bars and splits table of the same
  store the signal was recorded in, graded through that store's latest session: entry on the next session by entry
  type; a later open through the stop or the target fills at that open; inside a bar the stop wins when both are
  touched; time exit at the close of session h; delisted names close at their last close. Horizons 5, 10, 20.
  Consequence of the engine's own rule, accepted in advance: a signal whose entry fill is already at or above the
  1R target is `entry_skipped` (`open_through_target`) and is not a trade.
- Statistic: `research.leaderboard.edge_stats` per (strategy, window, horizon) through
  `research.leaderboard.leaderboard`: executable filter, planned-risk R, per-stock round-trip costs from
  `research.cards.with_costs`, t on non-overlapping blocks of the horizon. Windows: `research.cards.WINDOWS`
  (2024-10-07 .. 2026-10-05 and 2017-01-01 .. 2024-10-04).
- Multiple testing: Harvey-Liu haircut with n_trials = (number of `@t1r` strategies) x 3 horizons x 2 windows, this
  family only. Every (strategy, window, horizon) look is logged with `log_board_trials`.
- Survivors (the leaderboard rule): a positive haircut Sharpe at the SAME horizon in BOTH windows.

## The one pooled test
All `@t1r` signals of all strategies pooled into one series, each signal weighted equally, 20-session horizon,
`edge_stats` with n_trials = 1, in each window.
PASS only if net R per signal > 0 AND block t >= 2.0 in BOTH windows. Otherwise FAIL.

## Reported, not criteria
- Each strategy's original net R (its own target, same ledgers, same statistic) next to its `@t1r` net R at 20
  sessions in each window, and the count of strategies whose net R rose or fell in both windows / one / neither.
- The pooled statistic for the original targets (in-sample contrast).
- A reproduction check: the same grading path with the ORIGINAL target must give back the stored outcomes on a
  sample chunk; the share of rows that match is reported. A mismatch is reported, not repaired.

## Limits stated before the result
- The ledger signals come from stores screened before the 2026-10-10 splits backfill: the universe filters ran on
  adjusted, not as-traded, prices, and those stores' splits tables are nearly empty. This regrade inherits that; the
  full re-run in progress is not used.
- Shadow grading is per signal, not a portfolio: no cash, slot or position limits, every signal weighted equally,
  overlapping signals in the same name all counted. A pass is not a tradable result.
- Daily bars: the intrabar order of stop and target is unknown, the stop is assumed first. With a target only 1R
  away this assumption bites much more often than with far targets, so the regrade is biased against the 1R exit on
  wide-range days; the target fill assumes a resting limit filled at its price with no extra slippage beyond the
  per-stock round-trip cost.
- For memory, bars are read up to 90 calendar days after the last signal of a chunk (20 sessions need about 30); a
  name with fewer than 20 bars in that span that later resumed trading is closed at its last close as delisted.
- The 2017-24 window is partly survivor-biased.
- Strategies whose own exits are trailing stops or `should_exit` rules are not modelled by the shadow ledger at
  all (original or regraded); only stop, target and time exits are.
- A survivor or a pooled PASS would send that exit to a portfolio replay and the walk-forward skill; it would enable
  nothing. Any change after the result (1.0R multiple, horizon, weighting, exclusions) is a new trial.
