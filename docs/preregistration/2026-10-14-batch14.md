## Card 1: `sp500_four_signal_composite`
1. Signal day: each month's last session from 2017-01-31; orders at the next open. Before the first order: SPY.
2. Universe: the 500 largest by `mcap_pit` with an as-traded close >= $10 and at least 273 sessions of bars.
3. Signals: momentum = (close 21 sessions back / close 273 sessions back - 1) / sample standard deviation of the daily
   returns over the 252 sessions ending 21 sessions back; gross profit / assets; minus net share issuance; book-to-market.
4. Each signal z-scored across the universe, winsorised at +/-3, z-scored again; composite = mean of the available
   z-scores with at least 3 of 4; Z = the composite z-scored once more.
5. Holdings: rank band, enter within the top 90, stay within the top 120, 100 names (`b9_books.select`).
6. Weights: `mcap_pit` x s(Z) (1 + Z above zero, 1 / (1 - Z) below), capped at 9% and 3 x the name's cap weight
   within the universe with batch 9's registered cap reading (`b9_books.caps_for`); unused weight in SPY.
7. Re-weighting: the target is set again only when a name enters or leaves, or a weight drifted (close to close since
   the last order) more than 25% away from this month's target, in relative terms; otherwise no order that month.
8. Descriptive: the four one-signal books (same rules, the one signal alone), the cap-weighted universe (monthly), and
   the pairwise correlations of the one-signal books' monthly returns minus the universe's.

## Card 2: `vol_scaled_momentum_replica`
1. Base book: batch 9's replica as registered (`b9_books.momentum_orders`), simulated over the pooled window.
2. On each month's last session: sigma = sample standard deviation of the base book's last 126 daily net returns x
   sqrt(252) (exposure 1 before 126 exist); e = min(1, 0.15 / sigma). The held exposure changes, at the next open,
   only when |e_new - e_held| >= 0.10: the stock sleeve is the base book's weights drifted to that day, scaled by e;
   the rest is BIL. A base rebalance trades e_held x the new base weights plus BIL. Before the first base book: SPY.
3. Descriptive: average exposure, share of sessions below 1, worst month against the unscaled replica.

## Windows and grading (docs/gates.md: Full-period test, Bill rate)
Cards 1-2: three simulations, **A** 2017-01-01 .. 2024-10-04, **B** 2024-10-07 .. 2026-10-05, **pooled** 2017-01-01 ..
2026-10-05; each starts with the latest book on or before its first session. PASS on either route: (1) in both A and
B, haircut Sharpe > 0, alpha against SPY > 0 and alpha t >= 2.638; or (2) the same in the pooled window with alpha above
zero in each of A and B. Each logs one trial (`prereg-b14`).

## Card 3: `premise_check_french_momentum_profitability`
Blend = 0.5 BIG HiPRIOR + 0.5 BIG HiOP, monthly from 1963-07 to the files' last month. CAPM: (blend - RF) on Mkt-RF;
alpha per year, OLS t and Newey-West (6 lags) t; subperiods 1963-07 .. 1989-12, 1990-01 .. 2016-12, 2017-01 .. latest;
each leg alone and the correlation of the legs' CAPM residuals. **Supported** if the full-window OLS alpha t >= 3.0 and
the alpha is above zero in each subperiod (reading fixed here: OLS t is the criterion, Newey-West is reported beside
it). Link to our books: correlation, 2017-01 .. the files' last month, of (blend - market return) with (the 50/50 of
card 1's momentum-only and profitability-only books - SPY), monthly; "consistent" if >= 0.5 with same-sign means.
