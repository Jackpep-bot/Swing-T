# Pre-registration: batch 9 (two fully invested, low-turnover weights cards)

Committed 2026-10-14 **before** any simulation of these rules exists on real data. The module was written and tested
on synthetic data only. Nothing below may change after the first run except a harness-only amendment (a bug that
stops the rules as written from running), written into this file before results are read.

Cards: `docs/proposals/swing-methods-2026-10/batch9/` (branch `claude/batch9-cards` @ 8f297f5). Both are WEIGHTS
cards, simulated by `swing_engine/research/b9_books.py` on the daily book of `swing_engine/research/weights_book.py`
(fixed target weights traded at the open; the engine's replay sizes by stop distance and cannot hold them).

| # | Card | Holds | Trades |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `sp500_momentum_index_replica` | 100 of the 500 largest by `mcap_pit`, weight = mcap x momentum score, capped | twice a year |
| 2 | `leveraged_sp500_trend_switch` | 100% SSO or 100% BIL | when SPY crosses its 200-session average by 1% |

**Card 1 was chosen knowing the live record of the index it copies (SPMO, 2015-2026).** A pass is therefore a
forward-test candidate only, never evidence on its own. Batches 5-8 results existed when this was written; they
steered the research thread toward fully invested, low-turnover books.

## Expectation, stated before the run
Card 1: 2017-24 alpha 0% to +3% a year with t under 1.5; 2024-26 alpha +3% to +8% a year, t near 1.5-2; deepest drop a
few points worse than SPY's. Card 2: return above SPY's in both windows with a deeper worst drop, alpha near zero after
its beta of about 2 while invested. **Expected result: both FAIL on alpha t** in at least one window; card 2 is to be
read on money made and drops first (the owner's terms).

## Store
`config/prereg_b9.yaml` -> `data/live/replay_b9.duckdb` (`scripts/build_b9_store.py`): the batch-6 store (live store
as of 2026-10-13, bars through 2026-10-09, plus 2016-24 bars for SVXY, IWD, IWF, BIL) plus Alpaca SIP daily bars for SSO
2016-01-04 .. 2024-10-06. Price-only bars: dividends are credited neither to the books nor to SPY (SPY's yield is
about 1.3% a year; momentum names and BIL's interest are left out the same way: BIL's price barely moves, so cash
earns close to nothing here). Before 2024-10-07 the store holds names still liquid in 2024 plus the delisted ingest:
survivorship bias in 2017-24 for card 1, in its favour.

## Card 1: `sp500_momentum_index_replica`
1. Reference day: the last session of February and of August. Rebalance: the open of the first session after the
   third Friday of March and of September (the index trades that Friday's close; next open is the registered
   deviation).
2. Eligible on the reference day: a close that day, at least 252 closes up to it, and a momentum value. Universe:
   the 500 largest of those by `mcap_pit` (close x point-in-time EDGAR shares, `data.fundamentals.join_share_issuance`),
   leaving out names whose `mcap_pit` exceeds 10,000 x their 63-session median dollar volume (unit errors).
3. Momentum value = (close on the last session at or before reference - 1 month / close on the last session at or
   before reference - 13 months - 1) / sample standard deviation of the daily returns between those two sessions.
4. z = cross-sectional z-score over the 500, winsorised at +/-3. Score = 1 + z when z > 0, else 1 / (1 - z).
5. Selection of 100: current holdings ranked in the top 120 stay (best first, at most 100); then new names from the
   top 80 by rank; then the rest by rank until 100.
6. Weight = mcap x score over the 100, normalised; each capped at the lower of 9% and 3 x its weight in the
   cap-weighted 500 (the 3 x multiple raised only as far as needed for the caps to hold 100%, see the harness
   check); the excess goes pro rata to the names under their caps until none is over.
7. No stops. A held name past its last bar is sold at its last close (a performance delisting at a 30% haircut,
   `data.delisted`). The store's data start in January 2016 allows the first selection on reference day 2017-02-28,
   so window A's book starts at the March 2017 rebalance (2017-03-20); window B starts holding the September 2024
   selection at its first session.

## Card 2: `leveraged_sp500_trend_switch`
1. At each close: SPY close vs its 200-session simple average of closes. The first state is SSO if the close is
   above the average, else BIL; after that the state moves to SSO only at a close >= 1.01 x the average and to BIL
   only at a close <= 0.99 x the average.
2. The book holds 100% of the current state's ETF. A window's first session buys the state at the close before
   it; each change is traded at the next open.

## Simulation, costs and grading (both cards)
- `research.weights_book.simulate`: target weights traded at the open; cost = dollars traded x the symbol's
  `research.costs.cost_table` cost (at least 10 bp a side) on the session before; weights drift between trades; a
  part not invested is cash at zero.
- Windows: **A** 2017-01-01 .. 2024-10-04 (card 1 from its first rebalance), **B** 2024-10-07 .. 2026-10-05.
- Daily net returns vs SPY's close-to-close returns on the same sessions: return per year, deepest drop, worst month,
  share of months up, Sharpe, alpha, alpha t, beta, turnover, costs.
- 2 cards x 2 windows = 4 trials logged to `data/trials.jsonl` with tag `prereg-b9`.

## Harness check before the run (counts only; no book was simulated and no return was computed)
2026-10-14, on `replay_b9.duckdb`, `momentum_orders` and `switch_orders` only (scratch script; no book simulated).
- The first check found weights adding up to as little as 0.1%: some filed share counts are off by about 1,000x
  (Alcoa's `mcap_pit` on 2019-08-30 was $3,300 trillion, Hexcel's $5,200 trillion on 2024-08-30; also TXNM, VCEL, HL,
  CPK), so one name took almost the whole cap-weighted universe. Fixed before any run: a name whose `mcap_pit` is
  more than 10,000 x its median daily dollar volume over 63 sessions is dropped from the universe that day (real
  large caps sit near 50-2,000; the unit errors near 30,000-1,000,000). The same bad counts sit in every earlier
  card that ranks by `mcap_pit` (batches 3, 5, 6, 8); only the ranks of a handful of names move there, and those
  results are not re-run.
- The second check showed weights summing to 1 but single names above 9%: when the 100 names' 3 x cap weights add up
  to less than 100% (common: momentum leaders are often mid-sized), the cap rule cannot hold a full book. Registered
  reading, also fixed before any run: the 9% cap stays; the 3 x multiple is raised to the smallest value at which
  the caps hold 100% (`caps_for`).
- Final counts: 20 rebalances from 2017-03-20 to 2026-09-21, each 100 names summing to 100%, largest weight 6.6-9%,
  29-77 new names a rebalance (about half the book, as the card expects). Trend switch: 25 orders in window A
  (the first buy and 24 switches), 5 in window B.

## Pass rule
Over this group's **2 trials** (one per card). PASS needs, in both windows: haircut Sharpe > 0, alpha against SPY > 0
and alpha t >= `prereg_eval.alpha_t_critical(2)` (about 2.24). Card 1 passing earns a forward test only (see above).
Run once by `uv run python -m swing_engine.research.b9_books --settings config/prereg_b9.yaml` (results in
`docs/preregistration/2026-10-14-batch9-results.md`).
