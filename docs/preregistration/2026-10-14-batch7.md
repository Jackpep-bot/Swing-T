Committed 2026-10-14 **before** any replay of these strategies exists on real data. The modules were written and
tested on synthetic panels only. Nothing below may change after the first replay except a harness-only amendment (a
bug that stops the rules as written from running), written into this file before results are read.

states a deviation from the card, the module is the registered rule.

## Expectation, stated before the run
Card 1: many small gains and a few 15-30% breaks; net about +2% to +5% a year on the capital in use, beta 0.1-0.3,
alpha t near 1. Card 2: tens of signals a year, about zero to +1% net per trade. **Expected result: both FAIL the
rule below**; card 1 is the first card here whose return should not track the market, which is what it is for.

## Store and events
`config/prereg_b7.yaml` -> `data/live/replay_b7.duckdb`, built once by `scripts/build_b7_store.py` and copied per
job: the batch-6 store (live store as of 2026-10-13 plus ETF history and `sic`) plus table `form_events`, every
DEFM14A, SC TO-T (and its 2024 name SCHEDULE TO-T) and 424B1-8 filing in the EDGAR submissions pages of the 6,136
CIKs the 8-K ingest resolved, from 2015 on (`data/b7.py`). A filing's session is the first session whose close can
react to its acceptance time, as for 8-K items. `data.b7.join_b7` marks, only on this store:
- `deal_event`: a DEFM14A or SC TO-T on the company with no such filing in the 365 calendar days before it;
- `offer_event`: a 424B4 or 424B5 with no 424B of any kind in the 90 calendar days before it.
Known limits, accepted now: a DEFM14A is also filed by an acquirer whose own holders vote on a large stock deal;
targets whose CIK the 8-K ingest never resolved are missing. Price-only bars, as in batches 5 and 6. Before
2024-10-07 the store holds names still liquid in 2024 plus the delisted ingest: survivorship bias in 2017-24.
A completed deal shows as the target's last bar; the replay closes the position at that close with the store's
delisting-reason haircut (`data.delisted`: 1.0 for a merger, a performance haircut otherwise).

## Readings fixed in the modules (each module's docstring has the detail)
- `merger_target_spread_capture`: eligible on the signal day with `mcap_pit` >= $300M, `med_dv_63` >= $5M and
  `roc_20` <= +30%. The card's close-based break rule (close <= 0.85 x entry) is the engine's stop at 85% of the
  signal close, filled during the session or at the open on a gap. 180-session limit; no trail.
- `seo_marketed_offer_rebound`: pressure = close of the session before the signal <= 0.96 x the close 6 sessions
  before the signal, and that 5-session return minus SPY's <= -3%; close >= $5, `med_dv_63` >= $2M,
  `event_avoid_filters` rules 1-3. The 7-session exit is decided at the 7th close and filled at the next open. The
  card's descriptive split without the pressure filter is not run.

## Harness check before the run (signal counts only; no price after a signal was read, nothing graded)

2025-01-02 .. 2025-10-31, no trial logged, no shadow rows; only signal counts and skip reasons were printed). This
path hands `signals` what a real replay does (the last 300 sessions with features attached on the whole panel), the
check batch 6 lacked.
- `form_events`: 613 DEFM14A, 596 SC TO-T, 5,756 424B4 and 40,425 424B5 filings in all (3.28 million rows, almost all

  3,020 424B5 reaction sessions.
- `merger_target_spread_capture`: 28 signals, 28 orders; 6 entries skipped as `open_through_limit` (the next open was
  above the sizer's entry limit, typically the deal price jump).
- `seo_marketed_offer_rebound`: 68 signals, 68 orders; 11 skipped as `open_through_limit`.
- No strategy failure on any session.

## Pass rule
Over this group's **2 trials**. PASS needs, in both windows,
1. haircut Sharpe > 0 and net R per trade > 0;
2. alpha against buy-and-hold SPY > 0 with alpha t >= `prereg_eval.alpha_t_critical(2)` (two-sided Bonferroni, 5%
   over 2 trials; about 2.24).
Alpha against IWM is reported beside it for reading, not as a criterion.

## Beside the verdict (owner's terms, 2026-10-13)
Return per year, share of months up, deepest drop (`research.income_view`). A card that fails but made money
steadily with small drops in 2024-26 is listed as a **forward-test candidate** (shadow ledger first, paper at a
quarter of normal risk if the live sizer can trade it). That list is a judgment made after seeing results and is
labelled so; it is not a pass.
