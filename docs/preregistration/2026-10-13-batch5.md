# Pre-registration: batch 5 (15 methods from online traders and published event studies)

Committed 2026-10-13 **before** any replay of these strategies exists on real data. The modules were written and
tested on synthetic panels only. Nothing below may change after the first replay except a harness-only amendment (a
bug that stops the rules as written from running), written into this file before results are read.

Cards: `docs/proposals/swing-methods-2026-10/batch5/` (research thread "Trader strategy sweep", 49 ideas checked, 15
strategies and one filter kept). The rule of each strategy is the "Engine version" section of its card as coded in
`swing_engine/strategies/<slug>.py`; where a module's docstring states a deviation from the card, the module is the
registered rule. The filter card (`event_avoid_filters`) is not part of this group: it is graded on the small-cap
map's rows when that map exists.

## Expectation, stated before the run
The cards themselves expect little: none has clean, net-of-cost, post-2015 US evidence, and most trader material is
the seller's own claim. The research thread's stated expectations are about zero net for 12 of the 15. **Expected
result: most or all FAIL.** What is new is the universe: small and mid caps, which no replay here has covered.

## Universe and store
`config/prereg_b5.yaml`: the live store after the 2026-10-13 rename repair (copied per job), screened wide (every
name with close >= $1, $1M a day and 100k shares; about 6,400 in 2026); each strategy applies its own universe (`strategies/_b5.py`
SMALLMID = market-cap ranks 501-3,000 by `mcap_pit`, close >= $3, `med_dv_63` >= $1M, unless its card says otherwise).
Price-only bars: dividends are not credited, for the books or for SPY.

Market-cap ranks are taken among the screened rows that have a filed share count (`mcap_pit`; about 55% of them:
funds, ETFs and many foreign listings have none), so "rank 3,000" is near the bottom of that list.

Harness check before the run (2026-10-13, signal counts only, nothing graded): the joins and all 15 modules ran on
real bars for five dates without error. It showed that a 4,000-name screen could not reach the Russell cards'
rank 3,200, so the screen was widened to every name passing the floors before any replay.

Known limit: before 2024-10-07 the store holds the names that were still liquid in 2024 plus delisted names from
the delisted ingest. For small caps that is survivorship bias in the 2017-24 window, in the strategies' favour.

`execution.reuse_exiting_slots: true` so a rotation can refill its book:

Equal weight: `max_position_pct` = 100 / positions and a risk budget large enough that the cap always sizes. Equal
risk: 1% of equity at risk per trade on the strategy's own stop, capped at 100 / positions per name (Clenow's
0.1%-of-equity-per-ATR sizing is approximated by this; its card's "about 10 positions" is for a $10k account).
New orders per day = positions. Costs: the replay's cost model plus the per-stock spread top-up in the grader.

## Readings fixed in the modules (each module's docstring has the detail)
- `pre_earnings_runup_winners` decides its exit with two sessions to go, so the next-open sale is before the report
  (the card's day -1 close would sell at the open of the report day).
- `tax_loss_loser_rebound` holds 19 sessions per its rule text (the card's front matter says 15).
- Rank and percentile exits of the monthly and weekly books use the cross-section measured at the previous close.
- The two Bensdorp cards enter by limit; their profit targets are close-based rule exits (the replay would fill a
  price target on the entry bar itself). `sykes_first_green_day` enters at the next open (no close fill exists).
- `green_line_breakout_wish` draws peaks older than 250 sessions from the all-time-high close, not the high.
- `clenow_stocks_on_the_move_midcap`: a 10 x ATR(20) stop stands in for the card's ATR-based size; no fortnightly
  re-sizing. `piotroski_fscore8_small_value` scores 8 of the 9 signals, with trailing-twelve-month values built
  from year-to-date filings.
- `event_avoid_filters` rules 1-3 are applied inside `tax_loss_loser_rebound` and the Russell deletion card only.

## Order of work (fixed now)
1. Every card is replayed on **2024-10-07 .. 2026-10-05** (survivorship-free).
2. The 2017-01-01 .. 2024-10-04 replay is run only for cards that meet both pass conditions below in 2024-26. A
   card that misses them in 2024-26 is FAIL without it: the rule needs both windows.
3. The three once-a-year cards (two Russell cards, tax-loss rebound) have two or three events in 2024-26 and about
   ten over both windows, and cannot reach any significance. They are replayed on both windows regardless, report
   sign and size only, and can earn a forward test, never a pass.

Run by `scripts/run_batch5.sh` (waits for AC power and for the small-cap map to finish; tag prefix `prereg-b5-`,
results in `docs/preregistration/2026-10-13-batch5-results.md`, the IWM table in `...-batch5-results-iwm.md`).

## Pass rule
As batch 3, over this group's **15 trials**: PASS needs, in both windows,
1. the standard rule: haircut Sharpe > 0 and net R per trade > 0;
2. alpha against buy-and-hold SPY > 0 with alpha t >= `prereg_eval.alpha_t_critical(15)` (two-sided Bonferroni, 5%
   over 15 trials; about 2.94).
Alpha against IWM is reported beside it for reading, not as a criterion.

## Beside the verdict (owner's terms, 2026-10-13)
For every card the report also gives what the owner asked to see first: return per year, share of months up, the
deepest drop (`research.income_view`). A card that fails the rule above but made money steadily with small drops in
2024-26 is listed as a **forward-test candidate**: shadow ledger first, paper at a quarter of normal risk if it is
an ordinary swing signal the live sizer can trade. That list is a judgment made after seeing results and is labelled
so; it is not a pass.
