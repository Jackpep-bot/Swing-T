## Expectation, stated before the run (the card's)
Alpha about 0 to +2% a year, pooled alpha t under 1; likely FAIL. Added here: DivYieldST predicts the next month's
dividend, so held for six months most of its short-horizon content is gone; the book will tilt to quarterly payers that
went ex two months before each reference month.

## Our point-in-time versions of the two signals (reading fixed here)
- **DivYieldST** (Sign +1). Regular dividends (`special` false) from the store's Alpaca feed, split-adjusted to the
  bars' basis (`data.dividends.mapped`). Payer kind from the distinct ex-date months in the 12 months ending with the
  reference month: 1 annual, 2 semiannual, 3-5 quarterly, 6+ monthly (`dividends.MONTHLY_MIN_EX_MONTHS`; CZ keeps only
  quarterly / semiannual / annual codes, so monthly payers score 0). Expected dividend = the regular dividends with an
  ex-date in the month 2 (quarterly), 5 (semiannual) or 11 (annual) months before the reference month; yield = that /
  the reference-day close; buckets 0 (none), 1 (up to 0.5%), 2 (up to 1%), 3 (above 1%). Non-payers 0.
- **VolumeTrend** (Sign -1: a falling volume trend is the long side). Monthly share volume = the sum of daily
  (split-adjusted) volume per calendar month; OLS slope on the month number over the 60 months ending with the
  reference month, at least 30 months present, divided by the mean monthly volume. Bars start 2016-01, so it exists
  from the December 2018 rebalance; before that a name has 1 of the 2 signals, which meets "at least half".

## Rules
1. Reference days: last session of May and November; orders at the open of the first session after the third Friday of
   June / December. First rebalance 2017-06-19; before it, SPY.
2. Universe: as-traded close >= $5 and batch 9's `mcap_pit` guard, then the 500 largest by `mcap_pit`.
3. Each signal's percentile within the universe (ties averaged), oriented by CZ's Sign so 1 = the long side; score =
   the mean of the available percentiles, at least 1 of the 2.
4. Holdings: current holders stay while ranked within the top 150; then new names from the top until 100
   (`b9_books.select`, no entry limit).
5. Weights: `mcap_pit`, each capped at 5% (excess pro rata); fewer than 100 names: scaled to names / 100, rest SPY.
   Weights drift between rebalances. No stops; delisting at the last close x the store's multiplier.
