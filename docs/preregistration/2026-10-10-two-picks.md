## 1. `high_volume_return_premium` (Gervais, Kaniel & Mingelgrin, JF 2001)

Module `swing_engine/strategies/high_volume_return_premium.py`. Source read: the authors' working paper (Rodney L.
White Center WP 001-99, 17 December 1998), not the published tables.

| Rule | Value | Source / why |
|---|---|---|
| Universe | the replay's point-in-time universe (`settings.universe`: price >= $5, average dollar volume >= $5M, top 1,500 by dollar volume, as-traded through the splits table), refreshed every 5 sessions | engine. **Deviations:** all US listings, not NYSE only; no size groups (the paper reports small / medium / large separately); no merger / delisting / seasoned-offering exclusion |
| Formation day | every session (`as_of`) | paper section 7 / footnote 54 uses every day as a formation day; the main sample uses non-overlapping 50-day intervals |
| Interval | `interval_days` = **50**: the formation day plus the 49 sessions before it (reference period) | paper section 2 |
| Volume measure | daily dollar volume = close x volume (`dollar_vol`), split-adjusted store bars | paper: share volume x closing price |
| High volume | rank of the formation day's dollar volume within the symbol's own last 50 sessions >= `volume_rank_min` = **46** (top 10%), all 50 bars required; `pctile_50_of_dollar_vol` x 50, ties share the average rank | paper eq. (2) |
| Normal return | rank of the formation day's close-to-close return within the symbol's own last 50 daily returns between `ret_rank_min` = **16** and `ret_rank_max` = **35** inclusive (middle 40%); `pctile_50_of_ret_1d` x 50, ties share the average rank | paper section 4.3, eq. (7): the subsample with the larger returns and the only one whose limit-order cost test is clearly positive. Registered instead of the unconditional version; the unconditional version is NOT run |
| Price floor | lowest close of the 50 sessions (`min_50_of_close`) >= `min_price` = **5.0** | paper: no price below $5 in the 49-day reference period (here the formation day is included; split-adjusted close) |
| History floor | `hist_bars` (bars in the store up to `as_of`, with `pre_panel_bars`) >= `min_history_bars` = **252** | paper: at least one year of trading history |
| Direction | long the high-volume stocks only | long-only engine; the paper's low-volume short leg is not traded |
| Announcement filter | none | paper Table 7: excluding dividend and earnings announcement days changes little |
| Entry timing | signal at the `as_of` close, market buy at the next open (`EntryType.OPEN`) | paper forms at the formation-day close; the engine fills next open |
| Exit | time stop `max_hold_days` = **20** sessions | paper: 20-day test period |
| Stop | entry - `stop_atr_mult` = **3** x `atr_14`, static | engine choice (the paper has none; same as `pead_sue` / `earnings_seasonality`); the engine needs a stop to size |
| Engine overlay | `engine_trail = False` (no breakeven at +1R, no 10-day-low trail) | hold the test period, as the paper does |
| Target | none (`min_reward_risk` 0) | |
| Market regime gate | none | paper has none |
| Same-day ranking | `score` = volume / `avg_vol_50d` (largest volume shock first) | engine choice for the slot race; the paper equal-weights every high-volume stock |
| Columns read | close, volume, `atr_14`, `avg_vol_50d`, `pctile_50_of_dollar_vol`, `pctile_50_of_ret_1d`, `min_50_of_close`, `hist_bars` | all bar-derived; no EDGAR data |

## 2. `momentum_volume_early_stage` (Lee & Swaminathan, JF 2000)

Module `swing_engine/strategies/momentum_volume_early_stage.py`. Source read: the published article.

| Rule | Value | Source / why |
|---|---|---|
| Universe | replay universe (as above) | engine. **Deviations:** all US listings (the paper is NYSE/AMEX only and excludes Nasdaq for dealer double-counting; consolidated volume is used here for every venue); price floor $5 from the universe instead of the paper's $1; ADRs / REITs / funds not excluded beyond the universe's own screen |
| Rebalance day | last NYSE session of each month (`month_end` = 1, published calendar) | paper: sorts at the beginning of each month |
| Formation period | J = `formation_days` = **126** sessions (6 months), ending `skip_days` = **5** sessions before the signal day | paper: J = 6 is the focal case; one-week lag between formation and holding. The engine's next-open fill adds one more session |
| Formation return | close[t-5] / close[t-131] - 1 on split-adjusted closes; needs 132 bars in the 300-session replay view | paper: past J-month return |
| Formation volume | mean of daily `turnover` over the 126 formation sessions (bars t-130 .. t-5), every one of the 126 finite; `turnover` = volume / shares outstanding from `data.fundamentals.join_edgar` (latest XBRL share count filed on or before the bar) | paper: average daily turnover = shares traded / shares outstanding. **Deviation:** shares as last filed, not end-of-day shares; split timing between bars and filings is not corrected |
| History floor | `hist_bars` >= `min_history_bars` = **504** | paper: at least two years of data before formation |
| Sort population | the session's scanned rows (replay universe members) with a finite formation return, a finite formation turnover and enough history | paper: all eligible stocks |
| Winners | percentile rank of the formation return in that population > 1 - `winner_fraction` = **0.10** (top decile, R10) | paper: 10 return portfolios |
| Low volume | percentile rank of formation turnover in the same population <= `low_volume_fraction` = **1/3** (bottom tercile, V1); the two sorts are independent | paper: 3 volume portfolios, independent sorts |
| Entry trigger | rebalance day, winner AND low volume | paper: R10V1, the long leg of the early-stage strategy. The short leg (R1V3) is not traded |
| Entry timing | next open (`EntryType.OPEN`) | |
| Exit | time stop `max_hold_days` = **126** sessions (K = 6 months) | paper: K = 3, 6, 9, 12; J = 6 / K = 6 is the cell the text discusses. Only K = 6 is registered |
| Stop | entry - `stop_atr_mult` = **3** x `atr_63`, static | engine choice (the paper has none; same as `composite_cost_aware_rank`); the engine needs a stop to size |
| Engine overlay | `engine_trail = False` | hold the K months |
| Target | none (`min_reward_risk` 0) | |
| Market regime gate | none | paper has none |
| Weighting | the replay's sizing (below), not equal-weighted overlapping monthly cohorts | **deviation** forced by grading the strategy alone in the replay; a name already held is not bought again at a later month end |
| Same-day ranking | `score` = return rank - turnover rank | engine choice for the slot race |
| Columns read | close, `turnover`, `month_end`, `hist_bars`, `atr_63` | `turnover` comes from `join_edgar`; a panel without it gives no signals (no relative-volume proxy) |

Data coverage: `turnover` needs an XBRL share count (about 2009-2011 onward), so both windows are covered; names
without EDGAR share counts (many ADRs, some funds) never enter the sort. Before replaying, confirm the store copy's
cached `fundamental_events` table is current (a stale cache logs `edgar_events_cache_stale` and the strategy sees no
turnover, i.e. no trades): rebuild with
`uv run python -c "from swing_engine.data.store import Store; from swing_engine.data.fundamentals import build_fundamental_events as b; print(b(Store('data/live/replay_p2.duckdb')))"`.

## How it is graded

1. Portfolio replay of each strategy ALONE, its own exits, no router:
   `uv run swing --settings config/prereg2.yaml replay --start 2024-10-07 --end 2026-10-05 --no-router -s <slug> --tag prereg2-<slug>`
   `uv run swing --settings config/prereg2.yaml replay --start 2017-01-01 --end 2024-10-04 --no-router -s <slug> --tag prereg2-<slug>`
   (4 runs, run one after another in one store, data/live/replay_p2.duckdb). Settings: `config/prereg2.yaml`
   (extends `replay.yaml`), set before any run and the same book as the three-pick group because neither card names a
   book size: risk 0.5% per trade to the stop, max position 5% of equity, max 30 open positions, max 10 new orders
   per day, sector cap 30%, $100,000 start. These caps are NOT changed after any run. Positions open at a window's
   end are closed at its last close (replay `end`).
2. Costs: the replay's `CostModel` defaults (10 bp per side slippage, SEC fee and FINRA TAF on sales), plus a
   per-stock top-up from `research.costs.cost_table` (Abdi-Ranaldo half-spread, floor 10 / 20 bp) on store bars:
   per trade, extra = max(0, cost_bps(entry signal date) - 10) / 10^4 x entry notional + max(0, cost_bps(exit date)
   - 10) / 10^4 x exit notional (qty x exit price). The 10 bp already charged by the replay is not charged twice.
   The extra cost is booked on the trade's exit date and subtracted from the daily equity curve from that date on.
   (`research.prereg_eval.grade_run`, unchanged from the three-pick group.)
3. Report per strategy per window: trades; net R per trade = mean of (pnl - extra) / (qty x (entry price - initial
   stop)); t-stat of net trade R (mean / (sd / sqrt(n))); annualised Sharpe of the net daily equity curve
   (`research.metrics.sharpe`, 252); max drawdown of that curve; exposure (mean of the replay's daily
   market value / equity). Also: average holding days, share of net P&L from the top 7% of trades.
4. Grading command (after the four runs):
   `uv run python -m swing_engine.research.prereg_eval --settings config/prereg2.yaml --slugs high_volume_return_premium,momentum_volume_early_stage --tag-prefix prereg2- --n-trials 2 --out docs/preregistration/2026-10-10-two-picks-results.md`

## Multiple testing

- Harvey-Liu Bonferroni haircut (`research.metrics.haircut_sharpe`) on each window's annualised net Sharpe with
  **n_trials = 2** and years = daily returns / 252.
- Deflated Sharpe (`research.metrics.deflated_sharpe`) with **n_trials = 2**, n_obs = daily returns, the daily
  returns' skew and kurtosis, periods_per_year 252. Reported, not a pass criterion.
- These four replays are also logged to the trial log by `swing replay`; the leaderboard and the three-pick group
  are reported separately and are unchanged by this group's grading. Counting this group together with the three
  picks (n = 5) is reported alongside as information, not as the criterion.
- What "positive haircut Sharpe" means with n = 2 (my arithmetic from the formula): p_adj = 2p < 1, i.e. t > 0.674,
  i.e. an annual net Sharpe above about 0.48 in window A (500 sessions) and about 0.24 in window B (1,952 sessions).

## Pass criteria (each strategy on its own)

PASS only if ALL of:
1. positive haircut Sharpe (n_trials = 2) in window A AND in window B;
2. net R per trade > 0 in window A AND in window B.

Otherwise FAIL, recorded in the card's `## Empirical (replay)` and in `docs/STATUS.md`. A pass goes to the
walk-forward skill and gates.md; it does not enable anything.

## Known limits (stated before results)

- Both are long-only legs of long-short results, graded on raw (not market- or size-adjusted) returns: a positive
  Sharpe in a rising market is not evidence of the anomaly, and the paper figures closest to these books are small
  (HVRP long leg 0.29-0.41% per 20 days against its size group for medium / large firms, gross; low- versus
  high-volume winners differ insignificantly in the first year).
- HVRP: on a 1,500-name universe roughly 4% of names qualify on an average day (10% x 40% if the two ranks were
  independent), far more than 10 new orders a day and 30 slots held for 20 sessions. The result describes the
  largest-volume-shock subset chosen by the same-day score, not the paper's equal-weighted portfolio. The paper's
  own bid/ask test is negative for large firms.
- HVRP rules come from the 1998 working paper; the published 2001 tables were not read.
- Early-stage momentum: K = 6 means about four independent holding periods in window A; 30 slots can lock for 126
  sessions after the first one or two month ends; the paper's sample ends in 1995.
- Turnover uses filed share counts and consolidated volume across venues; the paper excluded Nasdaq.
- 3 x ATR stops are engine additions: positions stopped out before the time exit deviate from both papers.
