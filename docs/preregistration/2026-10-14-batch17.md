## Expectation, stated before the run (the card's)
Return about 7-10% a year against SPY's 13.6% price-only, beta 0.55-0.75, pooled alpha t under 1; likely FAIL. Premise:
full-window t above 3 plausible; 2017-latest most at risk; correlation with the card 0.7+.

## Card 1: `sp500_low_volatility_replica`
1. Reference days: last session of January, April, July, October; orders at the open of the first session after the
   third Friday of the next month. First rebalance February 2017 (2017-02-21); before it, SPY.
2. Universe: eligible names (as-traded close >= $5, at least 253 sessions of bars, batch 9's `mcap_pit` guard), the
   500 largest of them by `mcap_pit` (reading fixed here: filters first, then the 500).
3. Volatility: sample standard deviation of the last 252 daily close-to-close returns ending on the reference day,
   x sqrt(252).
4. Holdings: current holders stay while ranked within the lowest 130; then new names from the lowest volatility up
   until 100 (`b9_books.select` on minus volatility, no entry limit).
5. Weights: 1 / volatility, each capped at 3%, excess spread pro rata; with fewer than 100 names the book is scaled to
   names / 100 and the rest is SPY (never binds when the universe has 500). Weights drift between rebalances. No stops;
   delisting at the last close x the store's multiplier.
6. Descriptive: tracking error against SPY; correlation of monthly excess-of-SPY returns with batch 9's replica; the
   same orders simulated on the total-return store (`config/prereg_b9_tr.yaml`, dividends reinvested for the book,
   SPY and BIL alike), return and alpha, beside the price-only grade.
