## Expectation, stated before the run
The cards expect little: alpha against SPY of 0 to +3% a year with t under 1.5 for cards 1, 2 and 4, about zero net
for cards 3 and 5, and about zero for everything in 2024-26. No card has net-of-cost evidence after 2010. **Expected
result: all five FAIL the rule below.** What is new is the kind of selection: beta removed before ranking, signals
taken from other stocks, and one return source that is not stock picking.

## Store
`config/prereg_b6.yaml` -> `data/live/replay_b6.duckdb`, built once by `scripts/build_b6_store.py` and copied per
job: the live store as of 2026-10-13 (bars through 2026-10-09) plus
- Alpaca SIP daily bars 2016-01-04 .. 2024-10-06 for SVXY, IWD, IWF and BIL (the live store had them from 2024-10-07
  only; SPY and IWM already reached 2016);
- table `sic`: the `sic` field of each company's EDGAR submissions document (`data/sic.py`), 6,853 symbols with a

  cards.
`data/b6.py` joins `sic2` and the three factor proxies onto the panel, and only on this store.
Price-only bars: dividends are not credited, for the books or for SPY. The screen is batch 5's (close >= $1, $1M a
day, 100k shares); each strategy applies its own universe to those rows. Before 2024-10-07 the store holds names
that were still liquid in 2024 plus the delisted ingest: survivorship bias in the 2017-24 window, in the cards' favour.

## Card 1: `vrp_svxy_dual_signal`, as simulated by `research/vrp_overlay.py`
1. At each session's close: realised vol = 100 x sqrt(252) x the sample standard deviation of the last 10 SPY
   close-to-close returns; premium = VIX close - realised vol; contango = VIX close < VIX3M close (store table `vix`).
2. Target SVXY weight: VIX / 100 when in contango with a positive premium; VIX / 200 when in contango with a premium
   of zero or less; 0 otherwise. SPY weight = 1 - SVXY weight. No target on a session with a missing input.
3. The target is traded at the next session's open, and only when it differs from the weight last traded by more
   than 2 points of the account, or one of the two is zero and the other is not. Between trades the weights drift.
4. Costs: the per-symbol cost of `research.costs.cost_table` (at least 10 bp a side for these two ETFs) on the
   dollars traded in each ETF, taken from the signal day's row.
5. A window starts in cash at its first session's open (the book is bought there at the previous close's target).
   Daily return = close-to-close change of the book's value, net.
6. Windows: **A** 2018-03-01 .. 2024-10-04 (SVXY in its current -0.5x form), **B** 2024-10-07 .. 2026-10-05.
   2016-01-04 .. 2018-02-28 (-1x SVXY) is printed as a description and is never a trial.
7. Benchmark: SPY close-to-close on the same sessions. Reported beside it: return per year, deepest drop, worst day,
   worst month, share of months up.

## Books of the four engine strategies
One single-strategy replay per card and window (`research.prereg_run`, `--no-router`), each on its own book file
`config/prereg_b6_<slug>.yaml`, with `execution.reuse_exiting_slots: true` so a rotation can refill its book:

| Strategy | Max positions | Sizing |
|---|---|---|
| residual_reversal_ff3_etf | 50 | equal weight |
| within_industry_reversal_no_news | 50 | equal weight |
| large_shock_no_news_reversal | 8 | equal risk |
| industry_leader_lag_smallmid | 30 | equal weight |

Equal weight: `max_position_pct` = 100 / positions and a risk budget large enough that the cap always sizes. Equal
risk: 1% of equity at risk per trade on the strategy's own stop, capped at 100 / positions per name. New orders per
day = positions. Costs: the replay's cost model plus the per-stock spread top-up in the grader.

## Readings fixed in the modules (each module's docstring has the detail)
- Both monthly books: buy the 50 lowest scores, keep a holding while it is among the 100 lowest. The hold zone is
  the one measured at the close before the month end (the engine decides exits before it sees that session's
  signals), as in batch 5. A name inside `event_avoid_filters` is not bought and its slot is not refilled.
- `residual_reversal_ff3_etf`: factors are SPY, IWM - SPY and IWD - IWF daily returns; the fit has an intercept and
  the residual subtracts it; daily returns are not reduced by a risk-free rate. The fit uses the sessions that exist
  (at least 500 of 756), so the first scores of the 2017-24 window appear in early 2018; the book is empty until then.
- `within_industry_reversal_no_news`: the industry mean leaves the stock itself out; a name dropped for news or
  earnings still counts in its industry's mean; where the news table has no coverage, an 8-K stands in.
- `large_shock_no_news_reversal`: the 10-session exit is decided at the 10th close and filled at the next open (the
  engine has no close fill). The standard deviation is the sample one over the 60 sessions before the shock.
  A session the news table does not cover gives no signal. The card's "same rule with news" split is not run.
- `industry_leader_lag_smallmid`: "this week" is the last 5 sessions; the sale is decided at the 5th close held and
  filled at the next open. "Top 20% of industries" is a percentile rank above 0.8 among industries with at least 3
  leaders and 10 followers.
- `event_avoid_filters` rules 1-3 as batch 5 coded them (reverse split 252 sessions, Item 1.03 / 3.01 126, Item
  5.02 without earnings 63).

## Harness check before the run (signal counts only; no price after a signal was read, nothing graded)
2026-10-13, on `replay_b6.duckdb`, sessions 2025-09-02 .. 2025-10-31 (5,853 screened symbols): the joins and all
four modules ran without error. On 2025-10-31, 2,836 rows had `mcap_pit`, 3,893 a `sic2`, 5,768 a news flag.
- `residual_reversal_ff3_etf`: 50 and 43 signals on the two month ends; hold zone 100 names.
- `within_industry_reversal_no_news`: 50 and 34 signals; on 2025-10-31 only **36** names had a score at all. Few of
  the 1,000 largest go 21 sessions without a news item or earnings, so in such a month the book holds nearly every
  eligible name, not the worst laggards among many. The rule is tested as the card wrote it; this is a limit of the
  card's news proxy and is stated here before any result.
- `large_shock_no_news_reversal`: 116 signals on 31 of 43 sessions (about 700 a year; the card expected a few hundred).
- `industry_leader_lag_smallmid`: signals on 8 of 9 week ends, at most 30.
- Card 1: a target weight on 2,463 sessions since 2016, zero on 184 of them, 16% on average when on, 37% at most.
  SVXY's only daily moves beyond 30% are 2018-02-05 and 2018-02-06 (the real collapse, before window A), so the
  bars carry no unadjusted split.

## Order of work (fixed now)
1. Card 1 is simulated once on its windows A and B.
2. The four engine cards are replayed on **2024-10-07 .. 2026-10-05** (survivorship-free).
3. The 2017-01-01 .. 2024-10-04 replay is run only for engine cards that meet both pass conditions below in 2024-26.
   A card that misses them in 2024-26 is FAIL without it: the rule needs both windows.

Run by `scripts/run_prereg_batch.sh b6 2026-10-13 <slugs>` after batch 5 ends (tag prefix `prereg-b6-`; results in
`docs/preregistration/2026-10-13-batch6-results.md`, the IWM table in `...-batch6-results-iwm.md`, card 1 in
`...-batch6-vrp-results.md`).

## Pass rule
Over this group's **5 trials** (one per card). PASS needs, in both of the card's windows,
1. haircut Sharpe > 0, and for the engine cards net R per trade > 0;
2. alpha against buy-and-hold SPY > 0 with alpha t >= `prereg_eval.alpha_t_critical(5)` (two-sided Bonferroni, 5%
   over 5 trials; about 2.58).
Alpha against IWM is reported beside it for reading, not as a criterion.

## Beside the verdict (owner's terms, 2026-10-13)
For every card the report also gives what the owner asked to see first: return per year, share of months up, the
deepest drop (`research.income_view`; card 1 prints its own). A card that fails the rule above but made money
steadily with small drops in 2024-26 is listed as a **forward-test candidate**: shadow ledger first, paper at a
quarter of normal risk if it is an ordinary swing signal the live sizer can trade. That list is a judgment made
after seeing results and is labelled so; it is not a pass.

## Harness amendment (2026-10-14, written before `residual_reversal_ff3_etf` has any result)
The first 2024-26 replay of `residual_reversal_ff3_etf` made no signal at all: the replay hands `Strategy.signals`
only its last 300 sessions (`research.replay.SIGNAL_LOOKBACK_SESSIONS`), and the module fitted its 756 sessions
inside `signals`. The harness check above called `signals` on the whole panel, so it did not show this. The rule
is unchanged; the score moved to the panel feature `etf3_resid_rev_756_21_22_500` (features.extra, computed on the
whole panel before the replay slices it; tested against a direct regression and against a 300-session slice). The
card is replayed again under the same tag; the dead run's trial stays in `data/trials.jsonl` and its replay file is
kept as `...residual_reversal_ff3_etf.json.no-signals`. The other four cards' results had been read when this was
written; this card had none.
