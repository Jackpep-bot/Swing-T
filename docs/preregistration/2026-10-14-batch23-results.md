## Note found while reading (descriptive, no rule changes)
"No score tilt" matches R to the decimal because in R the score tilt never acts. When the 100 names' 3 x cap-weight caps
cannot hold 100%, batch 9's registered reading raises the multiple to the smallest value at which the caps add up to
exactly 1 (`b9_books.caps_for`). Every name then sits at its cap, so the weights are min(9%, k x cap weight) whatever
the score says. Checked on synthetic data: caps sum to 1.000000 and tilted vs untilted weights differ by under 1e-10.
The replica is in effect "top-100 momentum names at capped market-cap weights", and its shadow book is the same thing.
Batches 10 and 16 use the same construction.
